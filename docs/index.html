What It Does

Every Optimisation Your PC Needs
for Competitive Valorant

One app covers everything — no hunting through YouTube tutorials or Reddit guides.

🚀
One-Click Pre-Game Boost
Apply every optimisation at once before you queue. Kills background processes, sets power plan, flushes DNS, primes your PC.
⚡
System Optimiser
Disables Windows Update during gameplay, kills DiagTrack, sets Ultimate Performance power plan, frees RAM.
🌐
Network & Ping Reducer
Switches DNS to Cloudflare or Google, disables NIC power saving, tunes TCP Nagle algorithm and buffer sizes.
🔧
Registry Tweaks
Applies proven Windows registry performance tweaks for reduced scheduling jitter and faster system responsiveness.
🖱️
Mouse & Aim Optimiser
Disables mouse acceleration, sets raw input, calculates your eDPI, and prevents USB suspend interruptions.
⏱️
CPU & Timer Resolution
Sets 0.5ms Windows timer resolution, disables dynamic tick and core parking for consistent frame delivery.
🎮
GPU Optimiser
Disables AMD ULPS and Chill mode, toggles Hardware-Accelerated GPU Scheduling for reduced latency.
🎮
Valorant Config Editor
Edit GameUserSettings.ini directly in-app. Apply known high-FPS config presets with one click.
👁️
Visibility Enhancer
Adjusts AMD display brightness, contrast and gamma via registry for better enemy visibility without reshade.
🔊
Audio Optimiser
Disables Windows Sonic and sets MMCSS priority for lower audio latency and cleaner footstep audio.
🗂️
Startup Manager
View and disable startup programs that steal resources before Valorant even loads.
📊
Stats Tracker
Log your matches and track your stats over time to see the real-world impact of your optimisations.
